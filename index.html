About

I build software where correctness actually matters — most recently AI pipelines that turn dense clinical notes into care plans patients can understand and act on. Along the way I've become a little obsessed with the question behind the question: how do you know a generative system is good? That's led me deep into eval design, LLM-as-judge calibration, and the role domain experts play in defining quality.

Before healthcare AI, my projects ranged from fake-news intent detection research to flood prediction from news archives — the common thread is language, messy data, and systems people need to trust.
