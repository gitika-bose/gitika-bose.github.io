About

I'm an engineer drawn to building AI systems that actually matter to people. These days that's in search: I work on bringing AI to search at Bing in new and imaginative ways, rethinking how people find and make sense of what they're looking for.

I'm just as pulled toward healthcare AI and digital health, where getting it right isn't optional. Recently, I worked on Juno, software that turns dense clinical notes into care plans patients can understand and act on, and it got me questioning: how do you know a generative system is good? That's led me deep into eval design, LLM-as-judge calibration, and the role domain experts play in defining quality.

The common thread across everything I've built, from fake-news intent detection to flood prediction from news archives, is language, messy data, and systems people need to trust.
